Privacy Policy for Copyweave

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Copyweave, provided by Nir Barzilay, handles information when you use the Copyweave macOS application.

Copyweave is a local clipboard manager. It helps you search, pin, copy, and delete clipboard history, and provides optional on-device tools for rewriting, translating, and asking questions about selected text.

Copyweave is designed to keep clipboard content on your Mac or in your private iCloud storage when you enable synchronization. We do not operate a developer-controlled backend for clipboard content, and we do not sell personal information.

Who We Are

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

  • "App" means the Copyweave macOS application.
  • "We", "us", or "our" means Nir Barzilay.
  • "You" means the person using the App.

Information Processed by Copyweave

Clipboard Content

When you copy content while using your Mac, Copyweave may process and store supported clipboard items, such as text and images, to provide clipboard history. This content can include information that you copied from other applications. You control what you copy and can use the App's retention settings and app exclusions to limit what is stored.

Clipboard history is stored locally on your Mac unless you enable the optional iCloud synchronization feature. We do not send clipboard content to servers operated by us.

iCloud Synchronization

If you enable iCloud synchronization, Copyweave may synchronize saved clipboard history and pins through Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services so they can be available on your Macs. This data is stored in your private iCloud storage and is associated with your Apple account.

We do not have access to the contents of your private iCloud database. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices. You can control iCloud and remove synchronized data through your Apple device, iCloud, or Apple account settings.

On-Device AI Tools

Copyweave may offer tools to rewrite, translate, or answer questions about text that you select. These tools are designed to process the selected text on your Mac using available on-device system capabilities. Copyweave does not send selected text to a developer-controlled server for these tools.

App Settings and Technical State

Copyweave may store local settings needed to provide its features, such as retention preferences, excluded applications, pinned items, and interface preferences. These settings are stored locally unless a supported setting is included in optional iCloud synchronization.

Support Communications

If you contact us by email, you voluntarily provide the information in your message, including your email address and anything else you choose to include. We use that information only to respond to your request, provide support, and investigate reported issues.

Information We Do Not Collect

Copyweave does not require an account created by us and does not ask you to provide your name, telephone number, password, or payment information to use the App. We do not collect clipboard history, selected text, or app usage analytics on our own servers.

Copyweave does not use advertising networks, targeted advertising, cross-app tracking, or advertising identifiers for advertising purposes. The App contains no developer-controlled advertisements.

How Information Is Used

Information processed by the App may be used to:

  • Provide clipboard history, search, pinning, copying, and deletion.
  • Apply retention preferences and excluded-app settings.
  • Synchronize supported data through iCloud when you enable it.
  • Provide optional on-device AI tools.
  • Maintain the security, stability, and functionality of the App.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

Third-Party Services

Copyweave uses Apple's platform services, including iCloud and CloudKit when synchronization is enabled. Apple's processing of information is governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices:

  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Copyweave does not use a developer-controlled analytics, advertising, authentication, or cloud-backend service for the App's core features.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your clipboard content for advertising or profiling. We may disclose information that you voluntarily send to us when reasonably necessary to provide support, comply with law, protect rights or safety, or prevent fraud and abuse.

Your Choices

  • You can use retention settings and excluded applications to control local capture.
  • You can delete individual items or clear local clipboard history.
  • You can enable or disable iCloud synchronization in the App or system settings.
  • You can manage iCloud data through your Apple account and iCloud settings.
  • You can uninstall Copyweave to remove its local app data from your Mac.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored clipboard history and settings generally remain on your Mac until you delete them, clear the history, change retention settings, or uninstall the App.

Data synchronized through iCloud may remain in iCloud according to Apple's services, retention practices, and your iCloud account settings. You can manage that data through Apple's available controls.

We do not maintain a developer-controlled database of your clipboard history or a user account containing your personal information.

Security

We take reasonable steps to minimize unnecessary data processing and to keep the App's local data protected by the security features of macOS. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Copyweave is a general-purpose productivity application and is not directed specifically toward children. We do not knowingly collect directly identifying personal information from children through the App.

International Data Processing

Apple may process information through its services in countries or regions outside your country of residence. That processing is subject to Apple's applicable privacy practices and legal requirements.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality or the services it uses change. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. The updated policy becomes effective when it is published.
